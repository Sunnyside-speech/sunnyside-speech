Everything you need to know about online therapy and our clinic.

Virtual telepractice with young children blends direct screen interaction (interactive games, digital tokens, picture naming) with parent coaching. You'll be right beside your child or actively participating with their real toys and games at home, which makes the skills stick much faster in daily routine!
Yes! Melissa Wang is a fully Registered Speech-Language Pathologist (RSLP) in British Columbia. Most extended health plans (such as Pacific Blue Cross, Sun Life, Manulife, Canada Life) cover speech-language pathology services. After each session, you receive an official itemized receipt with license numbers for easy reimbursement. We also assist with BC Autism Funding Unit (AFU) billing.
Because Sunnyside Speech operates 100% online, we work with clients anywhere across British Columbia! While a large portion of our families reside in Vancouver, Burnaby, Richmond, Surrey, North Vancouver, West Vancouver, and the Tri-Cities, we warmly welcome families in Victoria, the Okanagan, and remote BC communities who need high-quality speech therapy.
It is a warm, casual phone or video chat with Melissa. You'll share what prompted you to seek speech therapy, your child's age and milestones, and what goals matter most. Melissa will share whether teletherapy is suitable and explain next steps—no pressure or commitment required.
No referral is legally required to see a Speech-Language Pathologist in BC. You can book directly with us at any time. (Note: A handful of extended health insurance providers may require a doctor's note purely for claims reimbursement, so please check your specific benefits plan handbook).
